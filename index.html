Dokkanle!

Dokkanle is a Wordle-style daily guessing game inspired by Dokkan Battle cards. Each day, players are challenged to guess a specific Dokkan Battle card based on parts of its passive skill, rarity, type, and leader skill. More information about the card can be revealed, making it easier to narrow down the possibilities.

Daily Mode

Guess a new Dokkan Battle card each day. Everyone gets the same challenge!

Unlimited Mode

Guess as many Dokkan Battle cards as you want. Test your knowledge endlessly!
