Dokkanle!

How To Play

Try to guess the Dokkan card with as little information as possible. The start of the passive will be shown initially and more will be revealed as the user clicks the "Reveal More" button. Once the whole passive is revealed, the rarity, type, and leader skill will follow. You can enter character names in the text box at the bottom of the page which will also display suggestions based on what you type. If correct name is submitted the answer will be shown on screen, but if it is incorrect a life will be taken away. If you do not guess the correct card within 5 guesses, you lose and the correct answer will be shown.

Extra Info

Daily resets at 8pm ET

Unlimited mode tracks your streak

The answer can only be a TUR or LR card.

The EZA/SEZA version of cards are always used.

Transformed version of cards are not yet added.
